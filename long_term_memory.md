### General Info

- Emily is an 18-year-old care recipient.
- Emily has autism and ADHD.
- Emily enjoys watching Pokémon and finds it relaxing.
- Emily enjoys drawing animals.
- Emily chooses drawing activities when given a choice.

### Sensory & Behavioral Triggers

- Emily gets scared by loud noises and can become very frightened.
- Emily may experience overstimulation from sensory inputs such as noise and lights.
- Anxiety or stress may occur in situations such as school or social environments.
- Emily has difficulty transitioning suddenly between activities.

### Calming Strategies & Routines

- Watching Pokémon can help Emily relax.
- Creative activities such as coloring, drawing, puzzles, or playdough can help Emily relax.
- Calming music and nature sounds may help with relaxation.
- A predictable daily routine with regular breaks may help Emily regulate.
- A quiet, sensory-friendly environment with minimal distractions may be helpful.
- Giving Emily a 5-minute warning before changing activities helps her stay calm and cooperate better.
